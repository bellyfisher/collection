The New York Islanders are a professional ice hockey team based in New York City. They are members of the Atlantic Division in the Eastern Conference of the National Hockey League.
Founded in 1972, the team has won four Stanley Cups in their history. They have yet to win any Presidents' Trophies.
The team is based in Elmont, New York, with two minor league affiliates:
- The Bridgeport Islanders
- The Worcester Railers
